8search app

Put 8search on your home screen.

Install 8search once and open it from its own icon — on iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows and macOS. No app store, no account, and no browser tabs in the way.

Your browser will show a short confirmation before adding the app.

8search is already installed on this device. Open it from your home screen, Dock or app list.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open 8search in Safari. This is required for “Add to Home Screen” on iOS.
  2. Tap the Share button, then scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Launch 8search from the new icon; it opens full screen without Safari’s toolbar.

Android

  1. Open 8search in Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap the browser menu, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. The 8search icon appears in your app drawer and home screen.

Windows

  1. Open 8search in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install 8search.
  3. Confirm. Pin the app to your taskbar or Start menu for one-click access.

macOS

  1. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar and choose Install.
  2. In Safari 17 or later, open File → Add to Dock.
  3. Launch 8search from Launchpad or the Dock. Keep it in the Dock for quick access.
Downloaded the .dmg and macOS blocked it?

That is Apple's standard check for apps from outside the App Store, not a virus. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, then confirm. Or run this once in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/8search.app

What the installed app keeps

  • Your own 8search icon and a clean, full-screen window.
  • Your signed-in session, preferences and guest history on that device.
  • Voice search, the camera, and the live globe.
  • Automatic updates whenever 8search is improved — no store update required.
