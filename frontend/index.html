Download 8search App

Fast, standalone search for Android, iOS, Windows & Mac.

Download 8search for macOS (.dmg)

Instant direct download · Standalone app · No app store needed.

Download 8search for macOS (.dmg)
1
Open 8search.dmg:

Double-click the downloaded 8search.dmg in your Downloads.

2
Drag to Applications:

Drag the 8search app icon into the Applications folder or your Dock.

3
Standalone App:

8search opens in its own window with zero browser bars or tabs!

Install 8search on iPhone / iPad (.mobileconfig)

✓ Direct 1-tap Home Screen Installer for iOS

1
Tap "Allow":

When iOS prompts "This website is trying to download a configuration profile. Do you want to allow this?", tap Allow.

2
Open Settings → Profile Downloaded:

Open your iPhone Settings app → tap Profile Downloaded (right below your name at the top) → tap Install.

3
Opens from Home Screen:

8search is now placed on your iPhone Home Screen. Tap the icon anytime to search in its own full-screen window — zero browser tabs, same as MacBook!

Alternative Safari Method:

In Safari, you can also tap the Share button (↑) → scroll down → tap Add to Home Screen (⊕) → tap Add.

Download 8search for Android (.apk)
1
Download & Tap:

Tap the download button above. When 8search.apk finishes, tap it to install.

2
Install App:

Confirm the install prompt on your phone.

3
Launch from Home Screen:

8search is added to your Home Screen and App Drawer as a standalone app.
